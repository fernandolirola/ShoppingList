**zezinho's Shopping List 1.2.0**

**Buying you can follow**
- The review screen shows each purchase live: queued, buying, bought, or why it failed (sold out, price went up, no money, no space, trader limit).
- The confirm button turns into a progress bar with a Stop button and what was spent so far.
- Sold-out offers are retried automatically if the replacement costs at most 10% more (both can be changed). Turn it off to choose Retry or Skip per item, or use "Try again" after buying.
- Dollars and euros are bought from traders when an upgrade or a purchase needs them.

**Plan further ahead**
- Each area has a level track: click a level to buy for every upgrade up to it, click again to unplan. Locked levels say what they need.
- "All available" plans the next level of every unlocked area, and Reset goes back to the start.
- Areas show their hideout icon, what their planned levels cost, and "Ready to upgrade" when you can build them now.
- Found-in-raid items you can craft are marked "Craftable", and crafts have a "Go to station" button.

**Cleaner list**
- The review screen is split by area. Item names, tags and prices are easier to read, and only statuses you act on are filled.
- New "Hide ready items" option. Unticked items and folded sections are remembered. Esc closes the window.
- The Christmas Tree only shows during the Christmas event.

Full list in CHANGELOG.md.
